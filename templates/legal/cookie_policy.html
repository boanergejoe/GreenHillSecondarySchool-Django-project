What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. They help websites remember information between requests, such as whether you are signed in or which preferences you have selected.

Cookies we use

  • Essential cookies: required for authentication, security, session management, and form protection.
  • Preference storage: your cookie choice is remembered in your browser so the banner does not appear repeatedly.

The platform does not currently use advertising or third-party tracking cookies. If this changes, this policy will be updated and any required consent will be requested.

Third-party resources

Some pages load fonts, icons, or scripts from external providers. Those providers may receive standard technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests their resources.

Managing cookies

You can accept or limit cookies using the consent banner. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may prevent you from signing in or using core features.

Updates

We may update this policy when our use of cookies or similar technologies changes. The latest version will remain available on this page.
