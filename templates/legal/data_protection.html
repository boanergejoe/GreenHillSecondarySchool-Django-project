Our commitment

{{ school_name }} is committed to handling personal information lawfully, fairly, and transparently. This policy applies to student, guardian, staff, applicant, and supplier information held in the management system and related school records.

Data controller and contact

The school is responsible for the personal data it processes. Questions, requests, or concerns should be directed to the school office using the contact details below.

Lawful basis for processing

We process personal data where necessary to deliver education services, manage enrolment and employment, meet legal obligations, protect vital interests, and pursue legitimate school administration. Where consent is required, it may be withdrawn at any time.

Data categories

  • Identity and contact details for students, guardians, and staff.
  • Academic, attendance, examination, and behavioural records.
  • Financial records, fee statements, and payment history.
  • Account credentials, access logs, and security records.
  • Safeguarding and welfare information where required by law.

Confidentiality and security

Access is limited by role and responsibility. Staff are expected to maintain confidentiality and use school records only for authorized purposes. The school applies technical and organizational safeguards, including access controls, secure authentication, and monitoring. Student blood-group and medical-note fields are restricted in the management interface to administrators and accountants and are not displayed in student, parent, or teacher profiles. Only health information relevant to student safety should be recorded.

Retention and disposal

Personal data is retained only for as long as necessary for educational, administrative, financial, legal, and safeguarding purposes. Records are then deleted, anonymized, or archived securely in line with applicable requirements.

Your data rights

Individuals may request access to their personal data, correction of inaccurate information, restriction of processing, or erasure where applicable. Some records may be retained or withheld where the law or safeguarding duties require it.

Data breaches

Suspected data breaches must be reported immediately to the school office. The school will assess the incident, take containment measures, and notify affected persons or authorities where required.
