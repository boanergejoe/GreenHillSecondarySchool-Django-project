Our safeguarding commitment

{{ school_name }} is committed to protecting every student from harm, abuse, neglect, and exploitation. The management system supports safeguarding by controlling access to student information and preserving accurate records, but it does not replace the school's safeguarding procedures.

Access to student information

  • Student records are available only to authorized staff, guardians, and students as appropriate.
  • Staff must access records only when there is a legitimate educational or welfare reason.
  • Confidential information must not be shared outside approved channels.
  • Guardians may access their own child's information, subject to safeguarding and legal restrictions.

Reporting concerns

Any concern about a student's safety or wellbeing should be reported immediately to the school's designated safeguarding lead or the school office. Do not investigate concerns independently or share them with unauthorized persons.

Responding to concerns

The school will respond promptly, record concerns appropriately, protect the student involved, and cooperate with relevant authorities where required. Information will be shared only with those who need it to protect the child.

Digital safety

Students and guardians should report harmful content, cyberbullying, inappropriate contact, or suspicious messages to the school. The school may preserve relevant records when responding to a safeguarding concern.

Review and accountability

This policy is reviewed periodically and should be read together with the school's safeguarding and child protection procedures. Staff are responsible for understanding and following those procedures.
