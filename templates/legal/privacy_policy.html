Information we collect

We collect information needed to operate the school and provide education services. This may include student records, guardian contact details, staff employment records, attendance, assessment results, fee and payment records, account credentials, and communications sent through the platform.

How we use information

  • Enrol students and maintain accurate academic records.
  • Deliver teaching, assessment, attendance, and reporting services.
  • Manage fees, receipts, and financial records.
  • Communicate with students, guardians, and staff about school activities.
  • Protect the security and integrity of the platform.
  • Meet legal, regulatory, and safeguarding obligations.

Who can access information

Access is limited to authorized users based on their role. Administrators, teachers, finance staff, students, and guardians see only the information needed for their responsibilities. We do not sell personal information.

How long we keep information

Records are retained for as long as required for educational, administrative, financial, and legal purposes. Retention periods may vary by record type and applicable law. When information is no longer required, it is securely deleted or anonymized.

How we protect information

We use role-based access control, authenticated accounts, secure session handling, encryption where appropriate, and regular system checks. No system can guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable steps to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

Your rights

Students, guardians, and staff may request access to their personal information, ask for corrections, or raise concerns about how information is handled. Requests should be made to the school office. Certain records may be exempt from disclosure where required by law or safeguarding obligations.

Children's information

Student information is collected and managed under the authority of the school and, where required, with guardian consent. We apply additional care to protect the privacy and safety of minors.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The latest version will always be available on this page.
