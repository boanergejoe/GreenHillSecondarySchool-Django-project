Purpose

This policy explains the standards expected from everyone who uses the {{ school_name }} management system. Access is provided to support teaching, learning, administration, finance, communication, and safeguarding.

Authorized use

  • Use only the account and permissions assigned to you.
  • Access only information you are authorized to view.
  • Keep records accurate and report errors to the appropriate staff member.
  • Treat students, guardians, and colleagues with respect in all communications.
  • Protect confidential information from unauthorized disclosure.

Prohibited activity

  • Attempting to access another user's account or restricted records.
  • Sharing passwords, session links, or confidential data without authorization.
  • Uploading malware or attempting to disrupt system security or availability.
  • Using the platform for harassment, discrimination, fraud, or unlawful activity.
  • Copying or distributing school records for unauthorized purposes.
  • Using automated tools to scrape, overload, or bypass platform controls.

Security responsibilities

Users must sign out of shared devices, use strong passwords, and report suspected security incidents promptly. The school may monitor activity for security, audit, and compliance purposes in accordance with applicable law.

Consequences

Violations may lead to account suspension, loss of access, disciplinary action, or referral to relevant authorities. The school may preserve and review records when investigating misuse.

Reporting concerns

Report suspected misuse, security incidents, or data breaches to the school office or system administrator immediately.
